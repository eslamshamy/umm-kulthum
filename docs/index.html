Umm Kulthum Atlas أطلس أم كلثوم

An interactive atlas of 151 places that hold the memory of Umm Kulthum (Kawkab al-Sharq, 1898–1975): the theatres, cinemas, palaces, streets and villages where she sang, lived, recorded and was remembered. Explore them as a 3D network of the people and songs they share, or on a globe down to the streets of Cairo. Each place can be read through three voices: Umm Kulthum herself, Pierre Nora and Henri Lefebvre.

Developed by Eslam Elshamy and Mohammed W. Fareed
Lab of Creative/Critical AI (LOCAI) · locai.carrd.co

Map tiles: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Music: concert and studio recordings streamed from the Internet Archive (archive.org). Built with three.js and d3.

AI narration

The atlas works fully from the dataset. The optional Seminar (Umm Kulthum · Pierre Nora · Henri Lefebvre) uses a language model. Every option below is free.
